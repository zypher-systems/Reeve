A new TUI: everything Reeve did, and what it cost, on one timeline.

## Update

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/reeve/main/install.sh | sh
```

## New

- **The ledger.** Home is one timeline of the conversation and everything Reeve did:
  - tool calls branch off Reeve's replies, with their result and receipt number;
  - a verified change is a bracket around its steps, closing on "verified · kept" or "failed · rolled back",
    with each undo inside it;
  - what reeved found and what the drafter drafted while you were here appear as rows;
  - on a wide screen, each model round shows its cost and the session's running total. Tool calls and checks
    cost nothing, and a draft is marked as the drafter's own budget.
- **Tabs.** Findings, orders, and memory are full screens now, and two screens are new:
  - **F4 spend:** a statement by day, week, or month, by role and model. `e` exports it to CSV, and `$` opens
    it from an empty composer.
  - **F6 system:** vitals over 24 h, 7 d, or 30 d, disks, what changed, and headlines.

  Switch with `F1`…`F6`, `tab` / `shift+tab`, a click on the tab, or `1`…`6` on a tab. `esc` goes back to
  the ledger.
- **`ctrl+k` searches everything:** drafted fixes, findings, orders, memory, receipts, tabs, and commands.
  `tab` asks Reeve instead.
- **Yes to the rest of the change.** On an approval inside a verified change, `a` approves its remaining
  steps. It ends with the change, and T3 still asks every time.
- **`ctrl+l`** redraws the screen.

## Changed

- **The default theme is ink** (warm charcoal). `[ui] theme = "brass"` in `~/.reeve/config.toml` brings back
  the original navy and brass.
- **The right rail is gone,** and `^b` with it. Its live numbers are in the line above the tabs and on the
  system tab.

## Heads up

- The ledger shows this session only. On the spend tab, an earlier session points to its `report.md`.
- In Konsole, `ctrl+shift+k` clears the terminal and Reeve can stay blank. `ctrl+l` brings it back.
