A new TUI: the whole machine on one board, and nothing useful a screen away.

## Update

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/reeve/main/install.sh | sh
```

## New

- **The board.** Home is eight tiles, one per function key:
  - **F1 needs you:** the approval asking now, with its keys, then the fixes the drafter wrote while you
    were away.
  - **F2 health:** CPU over the last day, memory, swap, temperature, network, and disks.
  - **F3 findings**, **F4 activity** (every receipt), and **F5 spend** (today by role and by hour).
  - **F6 changed:** packages, kernels, `/etc`, and services.
  - **F7 orders** and **F8 memory**.
- **Open a tile** with its F-key or a click. It takes the main area: a list and, beside it, the selected
  item in detail. The other seven fold into a **strip** along the top that keeps their numbers live.
  `esc` goes back to where you were.
- **F1 needs you** puts everything waiting on you in one queue: the approval asking now, the drafted
  fixes, and a kernel waiting for a reboot. `⏎` answers or runs the one selected.
- **F4 activity** shows every receipt full screen: what ran, its result, whether it can be undone, and its
  place in the hash chain. `u` undoes it, `v` verifies the chain.
- **F6 changed** lists what changed on the machine: package versions, the kernel, `/etc` files (with
  Reeve's own marked), and services.
- **`?` on any tile** asks Reeve about what's selected. The question says what it's about.
- **ctrl+k** and the other panels dim what's behind them.

## Changed

- **The chat** opens when you send something, or with `↑` from the board. It's the same timeline as
  before; tool results now line up on the right instead of trailing a dotted line.
- **Approvals:** on the board, an approval waits in F1 needs you, and `⏎` `a` `n` answer it from there.
  In the chat, it asks in the chat as before.
- **The default theme is slate** (cool near-black, filled tiles). `[ui] theme = "ink"` in
  `~/.reeve/config.toml` brings back 0.2.0's warm charcoal; `"brass"` is the original.
- **Keys:** `F1`…`F8` open the tiles (was `F1`…`F6` for tabs). `tab` / `shift+tab` walk the board and each
  tile. `^r` opens F4 activity.

## Heads up

- Tiles are drawn with half-block characters, which Konsole, kitty, foot, and most terminals draw edge to
  edge. With 16 colors or none, tiles get rounded borders instead.
- On a narrower terminal (under about 110 columns) the board uses two columns. Any tile that doesn't fit
  still opens with its F-key.
