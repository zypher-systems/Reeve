The observer reports quietly. A popup now means a proposed fix is ready.

## Update

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/reeve/main/install.sh | sh
```

The installer now restarts a running `reeved` on the new version.

## Changed

- **Findings no longer pop up.** They wait in `/findings`, the header badge, and the agent's view.
- **A popup means there's a decision to make:**
  - the drafter wrote a proposed fix ("Reeve has a fix ready: …"), or
  - a standing order stopped at its scope and left a proposal.
- **Standing orders that simply finish stay quiet** unless the order sets `notify = "after"`. The default
  is now `never`.
- **Opt-in:** `[observer] notify_findings = true` brings back popups for findings themselves, paced by
  `notify_every_minutes`.
