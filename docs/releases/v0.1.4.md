The state of the machine as a page, and a model list without models Reeve can't use.

## Update

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/reeve/main/install.sh | sh
```

## New

- **`/report`** (or `reeve report --days 1|7|30`) draws the state of the machine as one page and opens it in
  your browser:
  - **Headlines:** what needs you, worst first.
  - **Vital signs:** CPU, memory and swap, temperature, load, and network. Hover for readings.
  - **Disks:** growth per day, and when each will be full.
  - **What changed:**
    - packages installed, upgraded, or removed;
    - kernels, and a pending reboot;
    - services enabled or disabled;
    - `/etc` edits, with Reeve's own marked by receipt.
  - **Findings,** Reeve's verified and rolled-back work, spend by role, and what Reeve has learned.

  It's drawn locally from what reeved and the receipts already recorded: no model, no cost, nothing leaves
  the machine.
- **reeved keeps more history:** 31 days of minute readings (was 14), and a daily snapshot of packages and
  units, so "what changed" fills in over the coming days.

## Changed

- **`/model` and `reeve models` hide models Reeve can't use:**
  - models without tool calling;
  - image, speech, embedding, and batch-only models.

  `tab` in `/model`, or `--all`, shows them.
